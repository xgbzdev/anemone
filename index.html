Anemone

Looking for themes in /sdcard/Anemone/…

    Allow Anemone?

    To change your look, Anemone needs to:

    • Read your SD card to find theme packs
    • Preview wallpapers, icons and fonts from each pack
    • Apply them where your install allows it

    Android-style prompts from the OS may follow. You can revoke access in Settings › App Permissions.
